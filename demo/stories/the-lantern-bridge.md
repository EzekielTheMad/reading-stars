# The Lantern Bridge

An original Reading Stars demo story. Read this story before taking its quiz.

Mara and Leo were walking home from the village garden. Mara carried a yellow lantern. Its light made little circles on the path.

At the stream, they stopped. Three stepping stones led to the other side. The water was low, but the stones were wet.

“I will hold the lantern,” said Mara. “Then you can see where to step.”

A gust of wind swept through the reeds. The lantern went dark.

Mara hugged it to her coat. “Now we cannot cross.”

Leo did not hurry. He looked at the water, then at the sky. The moon had come out from behind a cloud. Its light shone on the tops of the stones.

“We can see them,” he said. “But we should go slowly.”

Mara tested the first stone with her boot. It did not move. She stepped onto it and waited while Leo followed. They crossed one stone at a time, leaving room for each other.

On the far bank, Mara smiled. She had thought she was the only one who could help because she had the lantern. Leo had helped by noticing something she had missed.

At home, they hung the lantern beside the door to light again tomorrow. Then they drew the three stepping stones on a piece of paper.

Under the drawing, Mara wrote, “A light can help. So can a friend.”
